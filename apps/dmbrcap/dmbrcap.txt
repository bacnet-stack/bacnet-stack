K.5.18 BIBB - Device Management-Backup and Restore-B (DM-BR-B)

This tool generates a Wireshark PCAP format file from a CreateObject services
encoded backup file.

To generate an Ethernet/SNAP capture containing one NPDU packet, use:

    dmbrcap --npdu "01 00 0c 0a"
    dmbrcap --npdu 01 00 0c 0a

To generate a capture containing one APDU packet with BACnet Ethernet/SNAP and
a default NPDU header, use:

    dmbrcap --apdu "10 0c 00 01"
    dmbrcap --apdu 10 0c 00 01
